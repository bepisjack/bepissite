what is this?
the bepis grand prix hosts weekly elimination style marble races that go live every Friday at 6pm, and allows users to bet their bepiscoins on the outcome!

what is bepiscoin?
when you register, you'll be given 5 bepiscoin to play with; however, bepiscoin doesn't come easy, so don't blow it all on your first bet! users will be given 2 every saturday.

how do I participate?
once you're logged in, select the racer you believe in and how many bepiscoin you're willing to wager! But be careful, you can only bet a single time each week, and cannot change it once you click submit!!
